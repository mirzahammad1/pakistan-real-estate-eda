## 🚀 Key Steps
1. **Data Understanding & Preprocessing**
   - Loaded dataset (prices, area, city, property type, beds/baths, description).
   - Cleaned messy columns (removed `PKR`, commas, standardized area units).
   - Removed duplicates & handled missing values.

2. **Data Cleaning & Consistency**
   - Standardized city names using fuzzy matching.
   - Normalized property types (house, apartment, plot, etc.).
   - Detected and treated outliers (IQR, Z-score).

3. **Feature Engineering**
   - Created `price_per_sqft`, log-transformed prices, and bedroom buckets.
   - Extracted posting year/month for time-based analysis.

4. **Exploratory Data Analysis**
   - Histograms & boxplots of prices, area, bedrooms, and bathrooms.
   - Correlation heatmap (numeric features).
   - Price comparisons by **city**, **property type**, **bedrooms**.
   - Violin plots & grouped insights.

5. **Insights & Recommendations**
   - City & property type strongly influence price.
   - Mid-range properties (5–10 marla houses) dominate listings.
   - Apartments rising in demand for rental markets in big cities.
   - Emerging cities (Faisalabad, Multan) provide investment opportunities.
